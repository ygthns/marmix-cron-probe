Custom CNC-Machined Natural Stone Wall Panels: Samples, Drawings and Quotes

Prepare a dimensioned drawing, a useful relief sample brief and comparable quotes for custom stone wall panels.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-13Updated 2026-11-222 min read

Send a dimensioned wall elevation

Mark the clear width and height, openings, external corners and adjacent finishes. Label preliminary dimensions as such. When attaching a photograph, explain what you want to carry into the design. Geometry, relief depth and stone colour are separate decisions for a manufacturer.

Specify what the sample should demonstrate

When requesting natural stone wall cladding samples, say whether you need a material chip, a full-scale motif or a small assembly. View the relief under the intended light and look at the junction between two motifs. A flat chip can show colour. To assess a repeat across joints, request a larger layout study.

Ask manufacturers to quote the same scope

Send each manufacturer the same drawing, approximate quantity, material preference and project location. Ask for separate details on drawing approval, sample charges, special cuts, spares, packing and freight. Confirm what starts the production lead time and where the supplier’s delivery responsibility ends. For a worked example with a wall elevation, sample approval and a ZIP code, see the US boutique hotel lobby quote request.

Discuss the material with the fabricator

Marble, travertine and Limra differ in veining, pores and tone. Ask about suitable piece sizes, edges and finishes for the specified stone and motif. Marmix collection reliefs can help define a visual brief. Confirm the feasibility and scope of bespoke machining for the actual project.
