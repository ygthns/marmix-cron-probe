How to measure and price stone for one entrance wall

Measure the net area of a small entrance accent wall, mark cut pieces and ask for a clear material quote.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-192 min read

Define the area you actually want to clad

If you want decorative natural stone or marble for one wall in an entrance, start with the full width and height. Mark doors, skirting and surfaces that will remain uncovered. A two-metre-wide, 2.5-metre-high wall with no openings covers five square metres. Where there are doors, corners or a specific panel starting line, a dimensioned sketch tells the supplier more than the area alone.

Put net area and order quantity on separate lines

Net area shows how much wall will be covered. Order quantity depends on module size, layout, edge cuts and any spare pieces you wish to retain. Explain that you are buying for one wall and ask for carton contents, required quantity and a spare allowance in writing. Our natural stone guide for a single wall covers material character and buying considerations.

Review samples upright in the entrance

Cratix grey travertine and Sonic Leylak white marble create different veining and relief effects. Compare samples beside the console, timber and flooring under the actual room light. The Natural Stone Institute discusses requesting representative samples to understand variation across a travertine selection.

Request itemised costs

Give Marmix your preferred collection, wall dimensions and socket or doorway positions. Ask for material, sample and freight costs separately. Discuss installation planning with your own installer. Our bespoke marble wall cladding quotation guide explains what information to provide for material pricing.
