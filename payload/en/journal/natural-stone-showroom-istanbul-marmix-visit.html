Visiting a natural stone showroom in Istanbul: what to compare at Marmix

Prepare for a Marmix showroom visit and learn how to assess stone texture, motif scale, colour and lighting before discussing your project.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-10Updated 2026-11-142 min read

Why visit a natural stone showroom?

A natural stone showroom visit lets you compare screen images with a surface at its actual scale. Marmix’s Instagram posts show different collections displayed side by side. When choosing natural stone panels in Istanbul, look beyond colour preference: motif scale, texture and viewing distance also affect the decision. Our natural stone sample guide sets out the points worth recording during a visit.

Prepare three references before you go

Bring a dimensioned sketch of the wall, an overview photograph of the space and examples of materials or furniture that will remain. If dimensions on the sketch are approximate, label them clearly rather than treating them as final order measurements. Our project brief guide explains which details help the first discussion. Confirm the current address and visiting arrangements through Marmix’s contact section before finalising your plans.

Look from a distance before moving closer

From a distance, the rhythm of a collection and its visual weight across a wall are easier to read. Up close, texture, veining and individual figures become more prominent. Comparing Anora motifs with Lumera’s colourful details from the same distance makes the two design approaches clearer. Liking one close-up does not mean the entire wall must have the same density; discuss alternative arrangements using your elevation.

Record the light and adjacent materials

Showroom lighting may differ from the light in your home or project. Note the conditions in which you saw a stone and ask about sample options. Our stone and stained-glass panels article helps you assess Lumera’s colourful details separately from its stone background. If taking photographs, record an overview and close detail without colour filters to make later comparisons easier.

Finish with a clear quotation request

Bring together the collection name, proposed cladding area, location and preferred motif arrangement. Following our guide to quotation factors, discuss material, layout and special details as separate questions. Ask for current information on installation, delivery planning, samples and any bespoke production conditions. These specific details provide a better basis for discussing how a showroom design could be adapted to your own project.

What should I confirm before visiting the Istanbul showroom?

Before travelling, contact Marmix to confirm the current address, visiting hours and whether the marble, travertine or Limra panel you want to see is on display. Bring wall dimensions, photos in daylight and evening lighting, and a flooring or furniture sample if available.

Questions about this topic

Does showroom lighting guarantee the appearance at home?

No. Light, adjacent colours and viewing distance can differ; assess a sample under your project conditions as well.

Do you need to place a final order during a visit?

You can first clarify the collection, dimensions and technical questions. Ask Marmix for current quotation and ordering conditions.
