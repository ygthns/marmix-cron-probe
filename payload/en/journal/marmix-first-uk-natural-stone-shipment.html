Marmix’s first natural stone shipment is on its way to the UK

Marmix has dispatched its first shipment of decorative natural stone products, designed and manufactured by Işık Maden in Türkiye, to the UK.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-182 min read

Designed in Türkiye, dispatched to the United Kingdom

Our first shipment of decorative natural stone products is on its way to the United Kingdom. From design and production to boxing and dispatch, preparing this order has marked a new step for Marmix. It brings the architectural surfaces we develop with Işık Maden in Türkiye closer to architects and interior designers working on UK projects.

Finding a new expression in natural stone

Each collection begins with the texture of the stone. Carved lines and figurative motifs bring out a different side of that material. On CNC-machined natural stone panels, the depth and repeat of the pattern shape how light moves across the wall. You can see Sesmari and Takot labels in the shipment photographs. Sesmari’s figurative reliefs and Takot’s linear and symbolic patterns show two different ways we work with Limra limestone.

From labelled cartons to wooden crates

We labelled the cartons by collection and pattern group. The photographs show the motif diagrams on the labels and the cartons arranged in wooden crates lined with protective material. The labels make it easier to identify pattern groups before opening a carton and to sort the collections on receipt. The gallery takes a closer look at the packing process.

Behind the shipment

Choosing natural stone wall panels for a UK project

Tell us where your project is based, the dimensions of the wall, the intended setting and the collections or motifs you have in mind. A drawing or reference photograph helps describe the desired colour and pattern. Since natural stone varies in tone and texture, start by looking at samples under the lighting you intend to use. Our guide to bespoke marble wall panels for UK projects covers material selection, and our CNC panel sample and quote guide explains what to include in an enquiry.

The start of our international journey

We design each surface around the character of natural stone and the space it can help shape. Our first UK shipment takes that approach, developed and manufactured in Türkiye, into an international market. We look forward to seeing where it goes next.
