How to test grazing light on travertine wall cladding

Use samples to compare texture, shadow depth and panel joints before choosing lighting for a travertine feature wall.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-132 min read

Stand the sample upright

Travertine cladding looks different laid flat on a table and standing against a wall. Orient the sample as the motif will appear on the wall. Compare daylight with the artificial lighting planned for the room. The pores and relief stay the same, while the shadows change how strongly the pattern reads.

Compare grazing and frontal light

Light the sample from the front to assess its colour. Then move the fixture to the side and observe the relief shadows. View the sample from the entrance and seating area so a dark line in one corner does not dominate your judgement. Hold planned wood and fabric finishes beside it.

Test the joint with two panels

One sample shows texture. Two panels side by side reveal how the pattern continues and how much grazing light emphasises the joint. Rotate one panel by ninety degrees and photograph both layouts from the same position.

Document the selection for a quote

Compare texture and motif scale in the Marmix Cratix and Anora collections. Include wall dimensions, light direction, collection name and preferred pattern layout when requesting samples. This gives the supplier a clear basis for the number and arrangement of panels in the material quotation.
