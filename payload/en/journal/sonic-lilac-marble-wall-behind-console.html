Marble behind a console: aligning Sonic Lilac with the furniture

Plan the console line, cladding edges and socket openings on one elevation when designing a wall with Sonic Lilac white marble panels.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-263 min read

Draw the top line of the console on the elevation

Start by marking the space the furniture occupies before laying out a ribbed white marble wall behind it. The height, width and two visible edges of the console give you clear references. Marmix’s Sonic Lilac collection offers eight motifs with horizontal and vertical relief lines and a panel size of 2 × 14 × 28 cm. If a row lines up with the top of the console, draw one layout in which the lines continue along it and another in which they shift by a row. For colour and texture decisions, see our guide to choosing marble cladding to complement furniture.

Work through both cladding edges

One edge may be more visible from the entrance and the other from the seating area. On the elevation, mark the panel widths and any parts of the motif cut off at the two ends. A centred console does not always need to share an axis with the cladding edges. For instance, if the pattern leaves a small sliver of stone near a door frame, shift the layout on the drawing and compare both the edge and the view around the furniture. Our guide to pairing timber and natural stone covers material transitions where the wall meets timber joinery.

Mark sockets and cable exits on the pattern drawing

Ask the project team to confirm the dimensions of any sockets, cable exits and access points behind the furniture, then plot them on the drawing. An opening through a ribbed panel can interrupt a line you liked on the sample. Record the panel number and opening location on the stone. Review the faceplate and stone edge with the project team before agreeing any cuts. If you are enquiring about bespoke CNC cutting, send Marmix the stone, motif and dimensions on the same drawing revision.

Review the marble sample beside the furniture

Place a Sonic Lilac sample close to the furniture finish and view it upright, as it would sit on the wall. Where possible, examine several pieces together to compare the colour and veining across the white stone. Stone Federation Great Britain distinguishes an indicative sample from a set that shows the stone’s natural range. Use our natural stone wall sample guide for the final review and share the console dimensions, wall elevation and motifs you would like to see with Marmix.
