How to approve bespoke stone wall cladding in a hotel mock-up room

Plan bespoke natural stone wall cladding for repeat hotel guest rooms with a mock-up, an approved sample and a room-by-room panel schedule.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-212 min read

Decide which wall will use stone

Timber veneer, wallpaper, fabric finishes and natural stone offer different ways to finish a hotel guest room. Patterned natural stone can be considered for a focal area behind a headboard or a console. The project team can evaluate acoustic and cleaning requirements alongside the interior design. See our guide to natural stone walls in hotel guest rooms for choosing where stone will be most visible.

Assess the samples in a mock-up room

Review sample panels under room lighting, in the orientation shown on the elevation and beside the furniture finishes. Compare Marmix Ligo’s black lines on white marble with a quieter stone sample. Look at the pattern from the entrance, then inspect the panel joint close to the bed or console. Stone Federation Great Britain discusses assessing internal stonework against project drawings, specifications and approved samples.

Prepare a panel schedule for each room type

Give standard, corner and accessible room layouts separate entries. Record the wall reference, clear dimensions, panel direction and any special pieces around furniture or sockets. Use the approved mock-up as the reference when reviewing colour and pattern in later rooms. Our guide to Ligo’s geometric lines has further examples of motif orientation.

Tie the sample and quotation to the same drawing

Include the approved sample reference, drawing revision, room count and preferred delivery window when requesting a quote. Mark any bespoke CNC pattern or dimension separately from standard panels. Ask Marmix to confirm sample availability, panel quantities and packaging scope. Our CNC panel sample and quotation guide helps turn the mock-up decisions into an enquiry.
