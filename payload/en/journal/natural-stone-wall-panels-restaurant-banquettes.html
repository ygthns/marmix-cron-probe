Choosing natural stone wall panels behind restaurant banquettes

Assess seated sightlines, lighting and visible pattern when choosing natural stone panels behind banquettes in a softly lit restaurant.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-171 min read

Check the motif at seated eye level

Mark the banquette back height, table level and wall lights on the elevation. Identify which part of the panel guests can see from the opposite table. If the banquette cuts across a motif, test a second layout with a different starting point.

Compare three stone samples in low light

Marble veining, travertine pores and the figurative relief of Sesmari Limra create different effects. Compare samples beside the upholstery under the intended restaurant lighting. The restaurant stone cladding guide covers material selection elsewhere in the dining room.

Specify the visible area for samples and quotes

Send the supplier the clear height above the banquette back, wall length and lighting positions. Mark motif direction and visible edges on the drawing. Verify cleaning conditions and the supporting wall with the project team.
