Custom CNC stone panels for a US boutique hotel lobby

A bid checklist for custom-patterned CNC natural stone wall panels in a US boutique hotel lobby, from a joint sample to delivery terms.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-192 min read

Draw one repeat for the reception wall

When sourcing custom-patterned CNC-machined natural stone wall panels for a US boutique hotel, draw the reception elevation and mark the main sightline. Show where the pattern starts and ends on each module. A photo conveys texture. A dimensioned drawing lets suppliers price the layout and cut pieces against a common brief.

Request a joint sample alongside a finish sample

Ask for a sample arrangement that shows the joint between two panels, the relief under the planned light and the usual viewing distance. Compare the travertine character of Marmix Anora with Ligo’s geometric lines as references for pattern selection. Confirm inventory, custom production options and sample lead times for the project. See our natural stone hotel lobby guide for deciding where the wall will be most visible.

Establish delivery and site responsibilities in the quote

Include the destination city and ZIP code, wall area, target date, packaging and order scope. Ask bidders to specify material costs, freight and who will receive the delivery. For a US import, review product and packing details with your customs representative. U.S. Customs and Border Protection provides official guidance on commercial invoice information.

What goes in an enquiry to a supplier in Türkiye?

Label inches or millimetres on the elevation. Show clearances around furniture, lighting and doors. A single pack with an image, dimensioned elevation and net quantity gives the supplier enough to start assessing a sample and quote. See the guide to sourcing stone wall panels from Türkiye for US projects for related procurement decisions.
