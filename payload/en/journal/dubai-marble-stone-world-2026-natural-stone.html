Dubai’s Marble & Stone World 2026 opens

Running 23–26 November at Dubai World Trade Centre, Marble & Stone World brings together natural stone, processing and installation solutions.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-241 min read

Four days for the stone industry in Dubai

Marble & Stone World opened alongside Big 5 Global at Dubai World Trade Centre on 23 November. Running until 26 November, the show covers everything from raw material and finished surfaces to processing machinery, installation and maintenance. The Emirates News Agency reported the opening on 23 November. Emirates News Agency (WAM)

What can designers and buyers see?

The organiser puts finished and unfinished stone, stone design and production equipment into the same programme. Architects can look at how a surface reads while buyers discuss how that same material will be cut, finished and maintained. In hospitality and commercial work, it helps to discuss how a finish will look at the actual scale of the installation alongside the small sample. Big 5 Global · Marble & Stone World

What should visitors take away from a stand?

For two products with a similar look, compare the trade name and origin, the batch represented by each approval sample, the available finishes and the lead time. Record the sample code and lighting conditions alongside on-site photos so samples from different suppliers are easier to distinguish later. The Journal’s guide to selecting a natural stone sample covers what to share with a project team.
