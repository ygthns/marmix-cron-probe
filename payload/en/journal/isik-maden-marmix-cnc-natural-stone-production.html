From Işık Maden stone production to Marmix CNC surfaces

Işık Maden is bringing its stone manufacturing experience to Marmix decorative wall cladding and CNC surface design.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-171 min read

What does the factory update show?

Caner Işık shared two factory photographs in an update covering natural stone manufacturing at Işık Maden and decorative wall cladding work under the Marmix brand. The announcement highlights CNC technology and original surface designs. It says more collection updates are planned. Collection names and launch dates were not announced.

What does CNC machining contribute to natural stone wall panels?

CNC machining helps specify a carved or relief motif in measurable terms. On an interior wall, the direction of the motif, the repeat and the panel joints are seen together. Review a sample of the intended stone to judge variation in veining and tone.

How do marble, travertine and Limra compare?

On marble, check how veining interacts with the relief. On travertine, look at the visible pores. On Limra, review how a pale surface and the motif read under the intended light. Compare the three samples under the same fixture and from similar distances. Marmix’s Sesmari Limra collection is one example of figurative relief on a pale stone.

How should architects review samples against drawings?

For a hotel lobby, restaurant feature wall or residential entrance, mark clear wall dimensions, doors, sockets and the visible edge of the last panel. Request a separate section if the motif wraps a corner. Two samples placed together can show how the pattern continues at a joint. Confirm fitting details and the substrate with the project team.

What belongs in a CNC stone cladding quotation request?

Send the supplier a collection name or motif reference, a dimensioned elevation, the piece count or area, and the delivery destination. Say whether the sample needs to show colour alone or a full-scale motif and joint. This gives you a consistent basis for comparing material, machining and delivery scope.
