Patterned natural stone wall cladding for hotel lift lobbies

Plan CNC stone panel layouts, door surrounds and sample reviews for a boutique hotel lift lobby.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-151 min read

Mark doors and floor indicators first

Before laying out panels for a hotel lift lobby, mark door frames, floor indicators, call buttons and signs on the elevation. Centre the motif on an uninterrupted area of wall. Identify narrow pieces beside doors separately and review them with the project team.

Evaluate pattern scale from the approach

Guests see the stone at different distances as they approach from the corridor and wait in front of the doors. A simple door surround and a patterned feature wall can help keep signs legible. The hotel lobby natural stone guide covers the separate question of placing a feature wall at the main entrance.

Review a sample alongside the door finish and lighting

Compare the figurative relief of Marmix’s Sesmari Limra collection with the door metal and selected lighting. Include the elevation, visible edges and motif direction in your sample request. Verify the trim detail around the frame, panel weight and substrate capacity with the design and installation team.
