Checking grey marble relief from three points in an office reception

Compare Torsion Grey panels from the entry, the reception desk and the waiting area. See what to include in a custom-size quotation request.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-273 min read

# Check which lines read clearly from the entrance

When selecting understated geometric natural stone panels for an office reception, assess the wall from three positions. Start by marking what a visitor sees on entering. Marmix’s Torsion Grey collection uses Rolex Grey marble in 2 × 14 × 28 cm panels and two motifs. Look at the horizontal and vertical wave reliefs in one sample group. From the entrance, compare a layout that runs in one direction with one that alternates in the area that remains visible. For the wider reception plan, see our guide to geometric stone cladding.

# Check relief shadows from the end of the desk

From the end of the desk, an oblique view can make the shadows of the relief more prominent. Hold the sample group upright and light it from the direction planned for the project. Keep the samples and light in the same positions while changing only the panel order. This makes it easier to see how the sequence affects the appearance. Our comparison of grey and green Torsion panels shows how different marble tones work with the same relief style.

# Check edges and colour from the waiting area

From seated eye level, note what the desk hides and which panel ends remain visible. Place several pieces of the same grey marble side by side to see the changes in veining and tone. Label the photos from all three positions with the panel sequence used. The Natural Stone Institute design manual is a general reference for stone selection and vertical-surface details. Our natural stone wall sample guide explains what else to look for when reviewing the pieces.

# Include the project dimensions in the quotation request

Send Marmix the clear wall width and height, the area you intend to clad and a dimensioned elevation. Mark inside and outside corners and any openings. Indicate which motifs should run in the same direction and which should alternate. For Torsion Grey, ask for separate details on standard panel quantities, project-specific cuts, sample availability and delivery scope. This helps you compare quotation lines against the same dimensions and panel sequence.
