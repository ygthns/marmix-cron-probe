Sourcing Custom Natural Stone Wall Panels for US Projects

Compare manufacturers for US projects by sample scope, fabrication lead time, crating and delivery to the job site.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-222 min read

What should you send a stone supplier?

When sourcing custom natural stone wall panels for a US project, include the destination city, interior application, wall drawings and estimated area. For a single residential wall, show door and corner details. For a boutique hotel, indicate where the pattern repeats. The natural stone project brief covers what to share with the first drawing.

Example US boutique hotel lobby quote request

Hello Marmix, we are pricing a 16 ft wide by 9 ft high lobby wall in Boston, MA 02108. Our elevation shows the reception desk, two outlets and the repeat of the CNC motif. Please quote grey travertine panels for this layout and a full-scale sample of one repeat for approval under the lobby lighting. Please itemise the stone, CNC work, sample, spare pieces, packing and freight. Where does delivery end, who handles customs clearance and duties, and who unloads at the hotel? Please state which drawing and sample approvals start the production lead time.

Compare travertine, white marble and cherry-red marble samples

Cratix grey travertine is a starting point when texture is important. Sonic Lilac White and Elazığ Cherry Red offer lighter and darker palettes within the same pattern family. At sample approval, record the stone tone, finish and how the motif meets adjacent panels. Use the natural stone sample guide to set approval criteria alongside the drawings.

Get the production and shipping scope in writing

Ask the supplier to state panel quantities, what cutting is included, which approval starts the estimated production lead time and the delivery point used in the freight quote. Clarify responsibility for packing, transport, unloading and import arrangements before ordering. Use the marble panel quote guide to review material, installation and logistics as separate items.

Compare supply scope for the same job site

Ask each manufacturer what sample they can provide, when production lead time starts, and whether the quote includes crating or delivery to the job site. Send the destination ZIP code and note unloading restrictions. Compare custom CNC-machined natural stone wall panels against the same drawing and finish brief.

How do you request samples and quotes for a US project?

Specify the material for your boutique hotel lobby, residential feature wall or showroom. Send dimensioned drawings and motif references for custom CNC panels, a texture reference for decorative travertine, or a layout for a two-tone marble composition. Ask separately about sample shipping costs and timing to your US ZIP code. Confirm when production lead time starts and who handles crating, customs and final delivery in the written quote.
