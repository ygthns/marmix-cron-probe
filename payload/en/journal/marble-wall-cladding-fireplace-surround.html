Choosing Marble Wall Cladding Around a Fireplace

Compare white marble, carved stone panels and travertine samples for a decorative fireplace wall by checking light, pattern and panel joints.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-182 min read

Identify the surface you want to clad

Mark the proposed stone area on a wall elevation. The decorative wall above a fireplace, the surfaces immediately beside the opening, and the floor in front are separate decisions. Before specifying stone or fixings near the opening, have the project team check the appliance manufacturer’s instructions and required clearances.

Compare veining, relief and light together

For an understated surround, start with a flat, veined marble sample. To make the wall a focal point, consider patterned marble panels such as Sonic Leylak White. For a more porous, matte appearance, compare Cratix Grey Travertine for colour and texture. Look at the samples from the seating area and from the entrance under the intended lighting. Light grazing the wall can emphasise both the relief and panel joints.

Draw the fireplace axis and panel joints

Mark the fireplace centreline, clear wall dimensions and the position of any shelf, television and lights. Compare starting the motif on the centreline with centring a panel joint there. This makes it easier to spot narrow cuts near the ceiling, under a shelf or at the edge of the wall while you are still reviewing samples.

Send dimensions and a reference image for a quote

Share the dimensions of the decorative area, your preferred collection, a photo of the wall and the proposed motif direction with Marmix. Include a dimensioned drawing if available. If you are considering a bespoke pattern, the CNC panel sample and quote guide explains how to specify the details you want to see in a sample.
