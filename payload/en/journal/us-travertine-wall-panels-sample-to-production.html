From travertine wall panel samples to production for a US interior

For a US interior project, link a decorative travertine wall panel sample to the production quotation, recorded motif orientation and approved range of texture.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-222 min read

Plan sample review alongside the shipping enquiry

When requesting decorative travertine wall panel samples from Türkiye for a US interior project, share the collection, preferred motif and destination ZIP code. Ask which physical samples can be sent, how they would be packed, what shipping covers and the estimated timing for the enquiry. Our guide to sourcing natural stone panels for US projects covers the information to include in an initial quotation request.

Ask for a range of textures

An initial piece conveys the general character of a surface. In a review before production, ask to see pieces with subtler and more pronounced pores side by side. Stone Federation Great Britain distinguishes an indicative stone sample from range samples that show variation in colour and texture. Marmix Anora shows framed relief motifs, while Cratix offers a grey travertine surface to compare. Confirm physical sample availability for your enquiry.

Record motif orientation and the accepted appearance

Record the sample ID, stone name, surface appearance and review date alongside the drawing revision. Where two motifs meet, include a view of the two pieces together in the approval record. The Anora patterned travertine guide shows how different reliefs can read on the same wall. Document the joints, tone and motif decisions separately.

Attach the sample decision to the production quotation

The project team can attach the approved sample photos and references to the quotation. Record the stone, pattern, panel count and any bespoke CNC dimensions against the same drawing revision. Discuss differences between the sample and available production with the supplier before ordering. Use our natural stone sample selection guide when reviewing the physical pieces.
