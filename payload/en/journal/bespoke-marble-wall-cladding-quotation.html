Bespoke marble wall cladding: what shapes a quotation?

How to compare material, pattern, quantities and supply scope when requesting a quotation for a bespoke marble feature wall.

MARMIX JOURNALUpdated 2026-11-14Updated 2026-11-223 min read

Compare scope before comparing price

For a marble wall cladding quote from Marmix, give the wall width and height, the dimensions of any openings, your preferred collection and the delivery location. A square-metre rate covers only part of the project. Ask which cutting, packing, freight and installation costs the quote includes.

A sample quote request to send to Marmix

Copy this example and replace the dimensions and delivery details with your own. “Hello Marmix, my living room wall is 320 cm wide by 250 cm high, with one door opening 90 cm wide by 210 cm high. Could you quote for the Sonic Elazığ Cherry Red collection, delivered to Kadıköy, Istanbul? Please confirm the panel quantity and specify which cutting, packing, freight and installation costs are included.”

Separate the stone from the layout

Material colour and motif arrangement are different decisions. The Sonic Vişne product page lists eight motifs and a piece size of 2 × 14 × 28 cm. These details provide a starting point for discussing a wall layout. A composition mixing colours or collections needs separate confirmation of compatibility and production arrangements; bespoke composition should not be read as a promise of unrestricted custom piece sizes.

Move from wall area to order quantity

Width multiplied by height gives an initial area, not a final order. Openings, niches, returns and the cutting plan affect the quantity required. Agree an allowance from the layout rather than assuming a universal waste percentage. Patterned pieces may be difficult to reuse after cutting, so confirm pack contents and coverage in the quotation.

Make the supply boundaries explicit

Ask how samples, packaging, transport, unloading and installation responsibilities are treated. Share the delivery location and target date, then seek written confirmation of the available arrangements. For a UK or US project sourcing from Turkey, agree shipping scope and any destination requirements directly. The collection image is a design illustration, not a costed completed client project.

Connect the showroom or sample discussion with your enquiry

When asking about a collection seen on Instagram, include its name and the detail you like rather than sending only a screenshot. Our showroom visit guide offers a checklist for narrowing colour and motif options. Share the outcome of an in-person comparison together with dimensions and the intended setting so the product and scope behind the quotation are clear. No current price or delivery commitment is published here; these need to be established for your enquiry.

Ask the same questions when comparing quotations

Use the same area, selected collection and delivery location for each quotation. State any special cuts or motif arrangement, and confirm the scope of transport, installation and samples separately. Check whether the price is expressed per square metre, piece or box and review the tax conditions on the quotation. Treat an approximate amount as provisional when information is incomplete. A clear scope helps identify potentially changing items earlier.

How do I get a price for a marble feature wall?

Ask for a written quote based on your dimensions. Send the wall width and height, openings, corners, project city and a reference photo of the stone or pattern. Compare fabrication, samples, delivery and installation as separate line items.

Questions about this topic

Can an Instagram photograph establish a final price?

A photograph alone does not define quantities, cuts, transport or installation; a written quotation needs measured project information.

Should quotations be compared only by price per square metre?

Different scopes can make that misleading. Check product, cutting, transport and installation separately.
