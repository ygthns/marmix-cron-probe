Design Notes

Travertine wall cladding for a considered living room

Explore a textured living room wall through Cratix, considering viewing distance, furniture, lighting and a clear project enquiry.

MARMIX JOURNAL2 min read

Choose one focal surface

Start with one living room wall rather than every surface at once. A television backdrop, a wall facing the sofa and an entrance view have different roles. On a TV wall, measure the visible stone separately from the area hidden by the screen. Natural stone wall design is about the balance of the whole room, so include shelving, artwork and furniture in the elevation.

Read Cratix at two distances

Marmix describes Cratix as grey travertine with 10 motifs and a piece size of 2 × 14 × 28 cm. Its organic recesses create a different reading from a flat finish. The texture of travertine is best studied both close up and from the seating position: details dominate at one distance, while repeats and shadows become more noticeable at another.

Bring furniture into the material study

Dark upholstery, a pale rug or a timber console can change the appearance of the same stone. Include fabric and flooring in your sample selection. Rather than matching every colour, decide which material should lead the room. A richly textured wall may work with fewer accessories in front of it, but test that direction within your own layout.

Plan the light alongside the surface

The direction of light changes the visibility of the recesses. Consider light and shadow on natural stone before choosing the final fittings. View samples in daylight and evening light, and assess screen reflections where a television is involved. Agree the final electrical and lighting arrangement with the project team.

Enquire with the actual dimensions

Send the Cratix collection name, wall dimensions, socket positions and an existing-surface photograph. Clarify quantities, cutting and installation responsibilities in the quotation. The cover is a collection presentation rather than a documented client living room. Confirm product-specific care and application requirements before ordering.
