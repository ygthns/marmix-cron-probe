Patterned Limra wall panels for the UK: samples and delivery quotes

Compare patterned Limra limestone panels, physical samples and delivery terms in one quote for a UK interior project.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-192 min read

Shortlist two patterned Limra samples

When sourcing patterned Limra limestone wall panels from Türkiye for a UK interior, start with two catalogue options. Marmix’s Sesmari, Takot and Lumera collections provide different motif arrangements to review. Ask the sales team which pattern and collection each sample represents, whether it is available and how it matches the product being quoted. Our patterned Limra specification guide covers the information needed for material and sample approval.

Approve samples under project lighting

Place the samples vertically in the orientation and lighting planned for the wall. On pale limestone, the relief can read differently as the angle of light changes. Compare two adjacent motifs to see the joint and variation across a run. Record the sample reference, drawing revision and approval date together. The UK’s Stone Federation Great Britain describes assessing internal stonework against the drawings, specification and approved samples.

Ask bidders to itemise delivery and costs

Share the clear wall dimensions, openings, estimated area, delivery postcode and preferred delivery window. Request separate figures for material, sample postage, packaging and the main shipment. The quote should name the delivery point and the parties responsible for import charges and unloading. Check product classification and any applicable charges using the UK Trade Tariff for the specific consignment.

What Marmix’s first shipment tells you

Marmix has dispatched its first shipment of decorative natural stone products from Türkiye to the United Kingdom. This gives buyers a documented shipment to reference when discussing a new enquiry. Sample availability, freight charges and delivery schedules are confirmed for each quote. Use our CNC panel sample and quotation guide to collect the project information you’ll need.
