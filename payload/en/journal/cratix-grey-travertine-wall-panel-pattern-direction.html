How to choose a pattern direction for Cratix grey travertine

Compare motif direction, joint lines and natural surface variation using physical samples of Cratix grey travertine wall panels.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-242 min read

Look at an individual panel and a group of panels

When you request decorative travertine wall panel samples, ask to see several pieces together as well as a single sample. Marmix’s Cratix collection comes in grey travertine, with 10 motifs and a panel size of 2 × 14 × 28 cm. A piece held close shows the pores and carved lines. Panels placed side by side show where the curves come together and where a joint interrupts the design. Compare the Anora patterned travertine collection if you want to review a different relief style.

Compare two layouts under the same conditions

Start by placing a few panels in the same direction. For a second layout, turn selected panels through 180 degrees where their dimensions and motifs allow. Photograph both arrangements in the same light and at the same distance. In one layout the curves may flow consistently, while the other may make the junctions more visible. Look at the changes created by the stone’s veining and pores as well. Stone Federation Great Britain distinguishes a single indicative stone sample from a set of samples that shows the range of colour and texture. Our natural stone wall sample guide covers what to check when viewing the pieces in person.

Check where the curves end at the wall edges

When transferring the panel order to an elevation, mark the wall edges, adjacent furniture and any openings. A motif that reads clearly in the centre can look crowded on a narrow piece at the edge. Compare the two elevations and see where the cuts land within each pattern. Keep any spacing shown in the sketch separate from the joint dimensions specified by the project team. Our guide to lighting travertine from the side explains how to compare the shadows cast by the relief.

Tell the supplier what you want to compare

Send Marmix the wall dimensions, the Cratix motifs you would like to see and the tones you want to compare. Ask about sample availability and request a photograph of several motifs side by side. If you are considering bespoke CNC machining, mark the motifs or dimensions you would change on your elevation. That lets you discuss the collection samples and the bespoke request separately.
