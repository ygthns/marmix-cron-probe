Comparing white and burgundy marble wall panel samples

Compare Sonic Leylak and Sonic Vişne marble panels under the same light, looking at veining, motifs and surrounding furniture finishes.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-192 min read

How do white and burgundy marble read together?

Catalogue photos are a starting point when choosing between two marble colours for an interior wall. Bring physical samples into the room where the stone will be used. Marmix Sonic Leylak white marble and Sonic Vişne give you two different stones from the same collection to compare. Where a motif is available in both stones, matching the sample design makes it easier to isolate colour and veining.

Test a dominant surface and an accent

Try white marble on the larger surface and burgundy in a niche or behind a console. Then swap them and compare both arrangements beside the furniture and floor. Vein variation becomes more apparent with several panels next to one another. Our guide to pairing marble wall panels with furniture relates stone colour to other finishes in the room.

Review daylight and evening light separately

Hold samples vertically and photograph them with the same exposure. A shared neutral background helps record the contrast between the stones. The Stone Federation Great Britain discusses viewing internal stone under lighting similar to project conditions. Use photos to document the comparison and use the physical samples to approve the colour.

Include both products in one enquiry

Share the collection names, motif preferences, wall dimensions and any furniture finish you want to match. Ask about the availability of corresponding motifs and physical samples in both stones. See our natural stone sample selection guide for reviewing colour and vein variation across more than one piece.
