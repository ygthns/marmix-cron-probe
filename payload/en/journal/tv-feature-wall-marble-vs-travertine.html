Marble or travertine for a TV feature wall?

Compare marble and travertine for the wall behind your TV, from texture and lighting to layout and care.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-222 min read

Which stone works better behind a TV?

For a TV wall with subtle grey tones and a porous texture, consider Marmix Cratix grey travertine. If you want visible veining and a lighter background behind the screen, compare it with Marmix Sonic Lilac White marble. Hold the two samples beside the TV with the screen on, then switch it off. Which surface draws your eye in each light? Our natural stone cladding guide shows how texture and motif scale vary across collections.

How do Cratix and Sonic look in the same light?

Cratix grey travertine draws attention to pores and shadows in the pattern. Sonic Lilac White creates a lighter composition, while Sonic Elazığ Cherry Red can add colour beside dark joinery. Look at both samples under side lighting in the evening with the TV on. Our guide to travertine interiors covers how the surface reads in daylight.

Plan the panel layout before mounting the screen

Mark the TV, sockets, cable outlets and media unit on a wall drawing. Check where panel joints will fall on either side of the screen. Show your installer any brackets, supports or cable routes alongside the proposed cladding. When choosing a natural stone sample, consider whether you can reach the finished surface for dry dusting and check that any care product is suitable for that finish.
