Planning a coloured marble feature wall for a villa living room

Compare stone samples, motif scale and panel layout when combining marble colours for a bespoke feature wall in a villa living room.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-192 min read

Start with the views of the wall

When choosing bespoke marble wall panels for a villa living room, identify the part people will see from the entrance and seating area. Mark the sideboard, sofa and window lines on the elevation. Moving a prominent motif out from behind a piece of furniture can change how the wall reads.

Compare two colour layouts in the same light

Try burgundy and green against a pale or grey stone ground. Use similar amounts of both colours in one layout, then let one colour dominate and reserve the other for accents in a second. The circles of different sizes in the Origo collection show how colour and motif scale can work together. Look at stone samples beside fabric and timber in daylight and after dark.

Check the pattern on a dimensioned elevation

Stone tone and veining can vary between pieces. As well as inspecting individual marble samples, look at how several pieces read together. Mark panel edges, sockets and narrow cuts on a drawing of the wall. If you are considering a custom pattern, the reference-photo-to-stone-wall guide explains what to carry from the image into the design brief.

Send two options when requesting samples and a quote

Give the manufacturer the wall width, height and position of any openings. Provide two colour and motif layouts and indicate which details are bespoke. Use Marmix’s Origo collection as a starting point, then consult the CNC panel sample and quote guide to discuss alternative stone or pattern combinations.
