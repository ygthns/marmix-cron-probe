Planning marble wall panels, sockets and reading lights in hotel rooms

Coordinate patterned marble panels, sockets and reading lights in one drawing. Check motifs, joints and faceplates against a hotel room sample.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-283 min read

Start with how guests use both sides of the bed

Custom wall cladding options for hotel guest rooms include timber panels, fabric-covered surfaces, wallcoverings and patterned natural stone. To evaluate marble, show the bed, bedside tables, sockets, switches and reading lights on the same wall elevation. The socket height matters, but so does how a guest reaches it. Does the table cover the socket? Will a charging cable pass in front of the lamp? Can guests control each reading light separately? Use our hotel headboard wall guide to compare materials and the elevation to agree on furniture and electrical positions.

Review where the pattern breaks on a sample

The black lines in Ligo’s white Kemalpaşa marble can read differently next to a dark faceplate. Positioning the faceplate between lines can keep the motif intact. Putting it over a line changes how the pattern continues across the wall. Hold the actual faceplate next to the sample and examine it from the bed and the room entrance. See our Ligo geometric stone wall examples for a closer look at the pattern.

Mark joints and openings separately

On the approval drawing, use different lines for the panel joints, pattern direction and openings for electrical accessories. Dimension the faceplate and the opening behind it separately. Ask the stone supplier and project team to review points near panel edges, the stone detailing and the box position before fabrication. The Natural Stone Institute guide to vertical stone surfaces provides general industry guidance on wall design and detailing.

Check both double and twin rooms

If you are considering the same marble collection for both room types, review each layout individually. As the bed spacing changes, check the motif alignment and switch positions again. When discussing materials, provide an elevation marked up for each room type, your chosen collection, the estimated panel count and the detail you want to see on a sample. Ask Marmix about Ligo or Sonic surface samples and a material quotation. Our bespoke CNC natural stone panel quotation guide explains what to include for comparable pricing.
