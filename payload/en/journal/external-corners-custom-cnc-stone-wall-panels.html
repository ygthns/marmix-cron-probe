Planning external corners for custom CNC stone wall panels

Plan motif direction, edge details and sample requests for geometric stone cladding at office reception corners.

MARMIX JOURNAL2026-11-142 min read

Draw both faces of the corner together

When patterned natural stone wraps an office reception corner, show the clear width of both walls and their shared corner on the same drawing. Mark the reception desk and entrance. Use both viewpoints to decide where the pattern begins and which direction it should read.

Decide how the pattern and edges meet

Relief lines can continue around the corner or stop at a plain edge. A continuous pattern emphasises the wrap, while a plain edge separates the two faces. Put both options on an elevation and mark the last cut panel and exposed edge. Confirm the cutting and installation details with the project team.

Ask for a two-piece sample before the quote

Include both wall dimensions, the collection, motif direction and a photo of the corner. Ask for a sample that shows the joint as well as the face of the stone. The CNC stone panel sample and quotation guide lists the other details to share with suppliers.

Compare collections under the same light

Review Torsion Grey and Ligo for motif scale, direction and colour contrast. View the sample alongside the desk finish from the entrance and waiting area. Record the selected corner detail and ask for the corner pieces to be identified separately in the material quotation.
